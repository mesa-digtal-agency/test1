Our Story

Crafted with passion,
designed for you

Founded in 2012, Lumière & Co. was born from a dream to create a sanctuary where every client feels seen, heard, and transformed. Nestled in the heart of the city, our studio blends Parisian elegance with modern artistry.

Our award-winning team of stylists are trained in the finest techniques — from balayage to keratin treatments — ensuring each visit is a bespoke, unforgettable experience.

Our Services

Each service is thoughtfully designed to give you the best results. Prices vary by hair length — book a consultation for a personalised quote.

From £45

Precision Cut

45 – 60 min

A bespoke cut tailored to your face shape, lifestyle, and hair texture. Includes a relaxing scalp massage and blowout finish.

From £120

Balayage & Highlights

2.5 – 3.5 hrs

Sun-kissed colour painted freehand for a natural, dimensional look. Includes toner, gloss, and blowout styling.

From £95

Full Colour & Toning

1.5 – 2 hrs

Complete colour transformation with professional grade pigments. Rich, vibrant results with shine-boosting gloss finish.

From £180

Keratin Smoothing

2 – 3 hrs

Brazilian keratin treatment that eliminates frizz and reduces styling time by up to 50%. Lasts 3–5 months.

From £55

Olaplex Treatment

60 – 75 min

Rebuild and strengthen broken hair bonds from the inside out. Ideal for chemically treated, damaged, or fine hair.

From £250

Bridal Package

Full day

Complete bridal experience including consultation, trial run, wedding day styling for bride and bridal party. Champagne included.
